Why we exist

Talent is everywhere. Visibility isn't.

Kalicoo began with a simple question: where are all the women shaping the future of technology? We knew they were there. We worked with them, hired them, recommended them and watched them build extraordinary things. But there was no single place making their expertise visible. So we built one.
