Lana Hopkins

Co-Founder & CEO, Seemira

Lana Hopkins is the Co-Founder and CEO of Seemira and a three-time founder with extensive experience building consumer technology businesses. Her work focuses on using technology to rethink customer experiences, commerce and the relationship between brands and consumers. Previously, Lana co-founded and led some.place, an immersive commerce platform connecting brands and communities through digital experiences, commerce and rewards. Earlier, she founded Mon Purse, the Australian fashion-tech company that pioneered mass-customised handbags using 3D technology.
