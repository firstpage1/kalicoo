Caroline Page

Founder & Director, Light Years

Caroline Page is the Founder and Director of Light Years, a strategic communications consultancy working with founders, executives and organisations navigating growth and change. Her career spans strategic communications, brand positioning, media relations and reputation across technology and emerging industries. Before founding Light Years, Caroline was Head of Communications & Media at Virtually Human Studio, the Australian technology company behind ZED RUN. She brings particular expertise in translating complex technology and emerging ideas into narratives that audiences, media and stakeholders can understand and engage with.
