Our Story

IT STARTED WITH A QUESTION.

Where are all the women shaping the future of technology in Australia?

The talent was there.
The visibility wasn't.

We knew they were there. We worked with them. Hired them. Recommended them. Invested in them. Watched them build companies, lead teams, create new technologies and shape industries.

But finding them wasn't always easy.

So we built Kalicoo.

Why Kalicoo exists

Making exceptional women impossible to overlook.

Kalicoo is a network for women building, leading and investing in AI and emerging technology.

We make their expertise more visible - to each other and to the organisations, investors, event organisers, media and leaders looking for them.

Because visibility creates connection. And connection creates opportunity.

Our signature network

100 WOMEN. ONE NETWORK.

At the heart of Kalicoo is 100 Women in Australia - a growing network of exceptional women shaping AI and emerging technology.

Not another awards list. A living network designed to make it easier to find her, know her and work with her.
